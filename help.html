How to use the online appointment system.

For clients: how to book

  1. Open the Book Appointment page.
  2. Type your full name, address and phone number.
  3. Type your car license number (number plate) and car engine number.
  4. Pick the appointment date. The mechanic list will update and show how many places each mechanic still has on that date.
  5. Choose the mechanic you want and press Book Appointment.
  6. A green message means your appointment is confirmed. A red message tells you what to fix.

Rules

  • Each mechanic can work on at most 4 cars per day. A mechanic marked FULL cannot be chosen for that date – pick another mechanic or another date.
  • A client can take only one appointment per day (we check your phone number and car license number).
  • The date cannot be in the past.

For the admin

  • Open the Admin Panel to see every appointment: client name, phone, car registration number, date and mechanic.
  • Press Edit on a row to change the date and/or the mechanic. Only mechanics with a free place on that date can be chosen.
